In August 2026 I took part in my very first Erasmus+ project, in Vilkaviškis, Lithuania. The topic was mental and physical health, and there were 42 of us from 7 countries.

## Getting in

I was really happy when I found out I had been accepted. I was at a Reformed (Calvinist) youth camp at the time, and had just gone for a run by Lake Balaton – where I had hitchhiked to.

## The Hungarian team

I met the rest of the Hungarian team at the airport, and we got on really quickly: six girls and one boy (me).

On the way home we were getting on brilliantly – we told each other really funny jokes all through the six-hour bus ride. The only thing funnier was at the airport, when they sprayed me with all sorts of perfumes, so I went up to a random person – who turned out to be Peruvian, and whose grandmother keeps guinea pigs in the attic. But that's another story…

## What we did

Most of the programme was community-building games. Every day a different country organised the morning and the afternoon activities.

What I liked most was the cultural night every evening, where we could try food and dances from the other countries. A smoking community also formed, led by the Greeks – but I didn't really join that one.

I spent the most time with the Georgians and the Spanish; there were also Italians, Greeks and of course Hungarians. Once I went running with one of the Georgians, which I really enjoyed.

The project's theme song was *I Love It*.

## Thank you

Our project leader, Vladas, was really kind – thank you for everything. And thanks to the Tudatos Ifjúság Alapítvány for giving me this opportunity.
