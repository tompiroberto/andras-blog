In September 2026 I spent ten days on Elafonisos, a small island off the southern tip of the Peloponnese, at an Erasmus+ youth exchange on digitalisation and AI. There were 37 of us from four countries: Greece, Romania, Portugal and Hungary.

## What we did

Most of the programme was about artificial intelligence – we even had a full Claude subscription to work with. We built websites (like this one) and made games, and in between there were lots of team-building tasks.

## Life on the island

We slept in a tent on a campsite, but we were well looked after: three big meals a day. There was plenty of free time, so we swam in the sea a lot.

The Hungarian team was great, and I spent a lot of time talking with the Portuguese – it would be lovely to visit them one day. I also learned the cup song, and a "snap-clap" game, where you keep the rhythm of a song by clicking your fingers and clapping.

I went running almost every day – never very far, but once I ran all the way round the island, and once I ran up to its highest point.

## Looking back

The ten days flew by. Our facilitator was really kind, and I'm sure this will stay with me for a long time.
