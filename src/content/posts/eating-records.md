I can eat a lot. My records so far: 12 *szilvásgombóc* (Hungarian plum dumplings) in my mouth at the same time, and a whole banana in 15 seconds.

The dumplings were free, of course – it happened at a training camp (a swimming and running one), where the food just kept coming. And that is the thing: if there is food, I can eat it. I simply love stuffing myself.

Also: I really like Nutella.
